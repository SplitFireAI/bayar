# BAYAR — Billing API for Your App Regions

For nearly two decades, there was only one way to sell digital experiences on iPhone: through a single gatekeeper, on fixed terms, with zero routing freedom. That era is over. Today, a historic shift is opening alternative payment rails across the **European Union**, **Japan**, and **Brazil**—putting choice, speed, and margin back where they belong: in the hands of the people who actually build the software.

Meet **BAYAR**. Built for **Bespoke App Yield & Alternative Routing**, BAYAR bridges Euros, Yen, and Reais into one clean, developer-first payment layer on top of Apple's `allowed-regions` entitlements. No bloated middlemen, no rigid checkout walls—just pure, borderless infrastructure that turns regional regulation into direct, autonomous revenue.

Not written by AI.
